About FinCalc Hub

FinCalc Hub is a small, independent collection of free financial calculators. Our goal is simple: help people make everyday money decisions with clear, accurate numbers — without accounts, paywalls or clutter.

How our calculators work

Every tool runs entirely in your browser using standard, well-established formulas. Loans and mortgages use the amortization formula M = P · r / (1 − (1 + r)−n); savings tools use compound-growth math. We show the full breakdown so you can see exactly how each result is produced.

Privacy first

We don't ask you to sign up, and the numbers you type are processed locally in your browser — they are not sent to or stored on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details, including how advertising cookies work.

Important disclaimer

FinCalc Hub provides general information and estimates for educational purposes only. It is not financial, tax or legal advice. Interest rates, fees and terms vary by lender and location — always confirm real figures with a qualified professional before making a decision.
