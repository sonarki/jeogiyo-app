About & Method

How our formulas work and why the numbers you see are reliable.

Calculators people use every day

Whether you're comparing loan offers, budgeting for a new car, planning a mortgage or watching your savings compound, a good calculator turns a confusing decision into a clear number. Every tool on FinCalc Hub runs instantly in your browser — nothing is stored, nothing is sent to a server, and you never have to create an account.

Why trust these numbers?

Each calculator uses the same standard financial formulas that banks and spreadsheets use — for example, the amortized payment formula M = P · r / (1 − (1 + r)−n) for loans. We show the full breakdown so you can see exactly how a result was reached. Figures are estimates for planning; always confirm the final terms with your lender.
