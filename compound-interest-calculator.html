The power of compounding

Compound interest means you earn interest on your interest. Each period, your balance grows, and the next period's interest is calculated on that larger balance. Combine this with steady monthly contributions and time becomes your biggest advantage — which is why starting early matters so much.

How to make it work for you

  • Start as early as possible — years matter more than amount.
  • Automate a fixed monthly contribution.
  • Reinvest returns so compounding stays uninterrupted.

Frequently asked questions

What is compound interest?

Interest earned on both your original money and previously earned interest, so growth accelerates over time.

How often should it compound?

More frequent compounding earns slightly more, but consistent contributions and time matter far more.

Do contributions really help that much?

Yes — regular contributions give compounding more principal to grow, often the biggest driver of the final total.
