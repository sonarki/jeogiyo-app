How your car payment is calculated

We start with the vehicle price and add sales tax, then subtract your down payment and trade-in to get the amount financed. That balance is amortized over your chosen term at the APR you enter, giving a fixed monthly payment. A bigger down payment or trade-in lowers both the payment and the total interest you pay.

Smart buying tips

  • Get pre-approved financing so you can compare it to the dealer's offer.
  • Negotiate the out-the-door price, not just the monthly payment.
  • Keep the term as short as your budget allows to minimize interest.

Frequently asked questions

How is a car loan payment calculated?

Price plus sales tax, minus down payment and trade-in, amortized over the term at your APR.

Is a longer loan cheaper?

Lower monthly payment, but more total interest — so it costs more overall.

Does a trade-in lower my loan?

Yes, it reduces the amount financed just like a larger down payment.
