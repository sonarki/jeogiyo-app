## How to use this bank

A hook is the first line your viewer hears (or reads) in the first 3 seconds. Platform data says ~71% of retention decisions happen there. This bank gives you 120 field-tested hook patterns organized by the psychological lever they pull. To use one: pick the lever that fits your product's buying motive, swap the bracketed slot for your product truth, and say it in the first shot — before any logo, any intro, any "hey guys."

**Pairing guide (product type → start with these chapters):**
| Product type | Primary chapters |
|---|---|
| Skincare / beauty | 4 Demonstration · 10 Skeptic · 2 Question |
| Supplements / wellness | 3 POV · 8 Problem-Agitate · 5 Contrarian |
| Tech / gadgets | 1 Bold Claim · 11 Value Reframe · 6 Curiosity |
| Food & beverage | 4 Demonstration · 3 POV · 9 Founder |
| Home / cleaning | 4 Demonstration · 6 Curiosity · 7 Social Proof |
| Pet | 3 POV · 7 Social Proof · 9 Founder |

Rules that make any hook land harder: say it in the first 3 seconds · one hook per video · burn it in as a caption too (sound-off viewers) · never stack two hooks.

## Bonus: The 3-Beat UGC Script Frame
Every hook above slots into the same 12-second frame we use in production:
1. **Hook (0–3s)** — one line from this bank, spoken to camera, caption burned in
2. **Proof (3–9s)** — one complete action: apply, sip, clean, demonstrate. Never cut mid-action.
3. **Payoff (9–12s)** — the honest reaction + one-line close ("That's the whole trick.")

Ship 20 variants, keep the 3 the algorithm loves. That's the entire game.
