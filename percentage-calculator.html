How to calculate percentages

Percent of a number: multiply and divide by 100 — e.g. 20% of 150 = 150 × 20 ÷ 100 = 30.

What percent: divide and multiply by 100 — e.g. 30 ÷ 150 × 100 = 20%.

Percentage change: (new − old) ÷ old × 100. Positive means an increase, negative means a decrease.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percent and divide by 100. Example: 20% of 150 = 30.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the first by the second and multiply by 100. Example: 30 of 150 = 20%.

How do I calculate percentage change?

(new − old) ÷ old × 100. A positive answer is an increase; negative is a decrease.
