How to measure cornmeal accurately

Medium grind. Coarser grinds weigh slightly more per cup. Because cups measure volume and grams measure weight, a kitchen scale is the most reliable way to get consistent results — especially in baking, where small differences matter.

FAQ

How many grams in a cup of cornmeal?

About 157 grams (5.54 oz) per US cup.

How many grams in a tablespoon?

Roughly 9.81 grams — a cup holds 16 tablespoons.

Is this US or metric cups?

This uses the US legal cup (236.6 ml). A metric cup (250 ml) weighs about 6% more.
