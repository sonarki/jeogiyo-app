How to measure brown sugar accurately

Measured firmly packed, the standard for recipes. Because cups measure volume and grams measure weight, a kitchen scale is the most reliable way to get consistent results — especially in baking, where small differences matter.

FAQ

How many grams in a cup of brown sugar?

About 220 grams (7.76 oz) per US cup.

How many grams in a tablespoon?

Roughly 13.8 grams — a cup holds 16 tablespoons.

Is this US or metric cups?

This uses the US legal cup (236.6 ml). A metric cup (250 ml) weighs about 6% more.
