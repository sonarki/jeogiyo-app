How to measure table salt accurately

Fine salt is dense; never swap 1:1 with kosher. Because cups measure volume and grams measure weight, a kitchen scale is the most reliable way to get consistent results — especially in baking, where small differences matter.

FAQ

How many grams in a cup of table salt?

About 273 grams (9.63 oz) per US cup.

How many grams in a tablespoon?

Roughly 17.1 grams — a cup holds 16 tablespoons.

Is this US or metric cups?

This uses the US legal cup (236.6 ml). A metric cup (250 ml) weighs about 6% more.
