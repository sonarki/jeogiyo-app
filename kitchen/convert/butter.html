How to measure butter accurately

1 US cup = 2 sticks = 227 g. Easiest measured by the wrapper. Because cups measure volume and grams measure weight, a kitchen scale is the most reliable way to get consistent results — especially in baking, where small differences matter.

FAQ

How many grams in a cup of butter?

About 227 grams (8.01 oz) per US cup.

How many grams in a tablespoon?

Roughly 14.2 grams — a cup holds 16 tablespoons.

Is this US or metric cups?

This uses the US legal cup (236.6 ml). A metric cup (250 ml) weighs about 6% more.
