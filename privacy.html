Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy explains how FinCalc Hub ("we", "us") handles information when you use this website.

Information we collect

The values you enter into our calculators are processed locally in your browser to produce results. We do not require an account and do not store your calculation inputs on our servers. We may collect standard, anonymous analytics such as page views and general location (country level) to understand which tools are useful.

Cookies and advertising

This site is supported by advertising. We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Your choices

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking cookies may affect how ads are shown but will not stop the calculators from working.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.
